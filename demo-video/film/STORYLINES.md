# Storylines

1. **One semester, one chat (built).** Real product footage follows two parties. A line Maya drops in the class chat ("I'm vegetarian lol") pays off in the trip plan. The film then explains the AWS pipeline and Supermemory, and ends on Pip.
2. **Pip's tour.** Pip narrates in character and carries the viewer from feature to feature, then walks into the AWS diagram. This is the strongest option for Best mascot, but it gives less clarity on AWS and Supermemory.
3. **Before and after.** A split screen puts a normal 40-message chat with no plan next to a Vibe party that ends with notes, tasks and a pick. It is punchy, but leaves less room for the architecture and Pip.
