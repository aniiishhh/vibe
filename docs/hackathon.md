## Rules that affect us

- Projects must be brand new and cannot be started before hacking time.
- Projects cannot be edited after hacking time.
- No plagiarism of other projects. The user has authorized integrating the earlier SameClass research tools. Preserve their attribution and record any organizer clarification about previously built services for submission.
- A Devpost write-up and evidence of work (GitHub repository) are required for judging. The repository must be public.
- Credit tutorials and AI assistance in the GitHub repository (README section "Attribution and AI assistance").
- Every team member registers individually and is added to the Devpost submission before the deadline.
- Video is optional: at most 2 minutes, on YouTube. State in the write-up whether we agree to inclusion in the closing playlist.

## Judging criteria (general)

1. **Originality**: unique problem or approach.
2. **Complexity**: problem difficulty, stack, technologies learned this weekend.
3. **Functionality**: does it work, and how much of it.
4. **Design**: visual appeal and user experience.
